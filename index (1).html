Frequently asked

Questions clients usually have.

Do you provide services across India?

Yes. The website is designed for remote, Pan-India support. The exact service scope depends on the client's requirement.

Is ₹599 the final price for every ITR?

No. ₹599 is a starting price for basic eligible cases. Complex returns, business income, capital gains, foreign income and other situations may require a different fee.

Can I send my documents on WhatsApp?

You can use the contact options provided, but avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. For important financial documents, use a controlled document-sharing process whenever available.

Will my return be filed without my confirmation?

The intended workflow includes review and confirmation of material details before filing, subject to the nature of the assignment.

Are the tax due dates shown on this website official?

No. Any dates shown on an illustrative page should not be treated as official. Applicable dates should be verified against current government notifications and the taxpayer's category.
